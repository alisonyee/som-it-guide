01

Day 1 Setup Checklist

  1. Activate your NetID. This is the single credential that unlocks almost every Yale system (email, Canvas, CampusGroups, printing, room booking, Wi-Fi). If you haven't activated it yet, do this first.
  2. Set up Duo. Yale requires two-factor authentication on top of your NetID password for most logins. Register your first device now; most systems below will prompt you for a Duo approval the first time you log in.
  3. Log into Outlook. This is where most official Yale and SOM communications will land going forward. Check for a welcome email from your Orientation Leader the week of August 10!
  4. Install Office. Not urgent, but while you're here, why not?
  5. Join CampusGroups. Most SOM and club events will live here. Download the mobile app, though there's a web version too.
  6. Join Slack. Before July, most students were invited to a temporary admitted-students workspace before being moved to the official SOM Slack workspace. Join the official one if you haven't already. Consider toggling notifications so the pings don't overwhelm you.
  7. Set up PaperCut. You don't need funds loaded on day one, but creating the account now means you're not scrambling to print your cheat sheet before your first midterm.
  8. Bookmark common links. You'll be navigating the same handful of links over and over during your first few weeks. See Section 11.

Accounts, Login & Security

NetID & password

Your NetID is your master login for nearly every Yale system — email, Canvas, CampusGroups, printing, EMS, and more. If you haven't activated it yet, do this as the first step!

YaleID & UPI

These are different from your NetID — a YaleID is a separate identification number, and a UPI is your Universal Person Identifier. You'll be able to pick up your student ID card starting Friday, August 14.

Duo (two-factor authentication)

Yale requires Duo on top of your NetID password for most systems.

  • Registering your first device: How to opt in to Duo Everywhere
  • New phone, same number: Activate Duo on a new device
  • iOS blank/gray screen: Fix guide

VPN (Cisco AnyConnect)

To access some specific resources (including the course auction when bidding for classes off-campus), download the Cisco AnyConnect VPN. Setup instructions here — use your NetID to log in.

03

Email, Office & File Storage

Install Outlook

Desktop (Windows/Mac):

  1. Open Outlook
  2. Select "Add Account"
  3. Enter your Yale email and NetID password and approve the Duo push when prompted

Web: go to outlook.office365.com and sign in with your Yale email and NetID password.

Mobile:

  1. Download the Outlook app
  2. Tap "Add Account" then "Office 365"
  3. Enter your Yale email and NetID password

Install Office

  1. Go to the Office Apps & Downloads page on Microsoft 365 (must be logged into Yale Outlook)
  2. Click "Install Office" — download on up to 5 devices (PCs, Macs, tablets, phones)
  3. Open the installer from your Downloads once it finishes
  4. Accept the prompt to allow changes to your device
  5. Log in with your Yale Office 365 credentials (standard Yale password) to finish

Navigating OneDrive / SharePoint

SharePoint is a shared-file platform, best for learning team documents or club-related tasks that need group-level access separate from your personal OneDrive. Access it at yaleedu.sharepoint.com.

OneDrive is your personal cloud storage, free with your Yale Office 365 account. Save files there to reach them from any device and keep an automatic backup of your work. Access it by logging into login.microsoftonline.com with your Yale email, then selecting OneDrive from the app launcher (top-left grid icon).

Campus Community Platforms

CampusGroups

Your go-to page for most SOM social and guest-speaker events — everything from the annual Business & Society musical to weekly club meetings to guest talks lives here, plus club sites.

  1. Go to the CampusGroups login page and sign in with your Yale NetID
  2. Download the app, select the "Yale School of Management" hub, and join the SOM Community Hub + Orientation channel with your Yale email
  3. Tap your account icon to add a profile photo and short bio
  4. Browse events on the Calendar/Events tab; click one and hit Register/RSVP
  5. Paid events (formals, Internship Fund Auction Night, Business & Society Musical) prompt for payment on the registration page; full events offer an automatic waitlist
  6. If you can no longer make an event, please unregister — it frees your spot for someone else (especially for free events)
Screenshots

Syncing events to your calendar

  1. Log into groups.som.yale.edu with your NetID
  2. Scroll to the bottom of your dashboard to "Synchronize my Events"
  3. Optional: check "Also synchronize my YaleSOM classes"
  4. Click "Enable Synchronization" (or "Manage Settings" if already connected)
  5. Choose Google Calendar or Office 365, log in, and click Accept on the permissions prompt
⏳ Sync can lag 48–72 hrs during peak weeks.

Slack

SOM relies on Slack heavily to make announcements about social events, club events, and student government updates. Peruse channels, but a few to know:

  • School-wide channels (#som-all-programs)
  • MBA-wide channel (#4055-mba)
  • Cohort channels (e.g. #2028-gold): formal cohort-wide announcements are often posted here
  • #som-marketplace, #free-food

Consider adding your photo to your Slack profile! It makes it much easier for classmates to recognize and connect with you.

A few things to keep in mind
  • Etiquette: react generously and keep posts kind — your posts and DMs are in a school-wide workplace
  • Notifications: manage notifications and mute channels as you see fit
  • Search: use the search bar to look across Slack or within specific channels; check out #som-faq and #som-bidding
  • Sections: use the "Sections" feature to group and condense channels by your own priorities

Printing

Print to one shared queue, then release the job at whichever compatible printer you're standing at — including many Yale printers outside SOM. Nothing prints until you release it. Quick-start guide →

Add funds

  1. Go to the PaperCut Web Print portal and log in with your NetID/password
  2. On the dashboard, click "Adding Funds" (you may need to log in again)
  3. Click "Add Funds" under the Yale NetID section
  4. Pick an amount ($5–$20 preset, or "Other" up to $99)
  5. Choose a payment method and submit
💵 Balances under $5 aren't refundable, but can be transferred to another student.

Submit a print job

  1. From the PaperCut homepage, click "Web Print"
  2. Click "Submit a Job"
  3. Select your target printer/queue
  4. Set your number of copies
  5. Upload your file(s) and submit

Release your print

  1. Once your job shows "Held in a queue," walk to any compatible printer
  2. Tap your ID card on the reader, or log in manually with your NetID, to release it

Room & Space Booking

EMS lets students book classrooms, breakout rooms, and interview rooms at Evans Hall.

  • Breakout rooms: located on the 2nd and 4th floors, intended for learning teams, leadership club meetings, case team practice, and other group use. Not intended for individual studying.
  • Interview rooms: intended for formal recruiting only; located on the 1st floor.

Booking flow

  1. Go to the Yale SOM EMS website, scroll to the bottom, and click "Sign In"
  2. Log in with your NetID
  3. Under "My Reservation Templates," pick a room category and click "Book Now"
  4. Enter your date, start time, and end time; click Search
  5. Pick an available room (shown with a green + and its capacity) and click the +
  6. In the "Attendance & Setup Type" pop-up, leave the default and click "Add Room"
  7. Click "Next Step"
  8. Fill in event name, event type, your group/cohort, and contact name/email
  9. Click "Create Reservation"

Managing & checking in

  • View or cancel bookings under "My Events"
🚪 Breakout and interview rooms require check-in (at the door or via email link) within 10 minutes of your start time, or the room releases automatically.

Campus Life & Transit Apps

Yale Rec App — PWG access before your ID card arrives

  1. Search the App Store/Google Play for "Yale Campus Recreation" and install it
  2. Open the app; tap the barcode icon labeled "Yale Rec ID" in the top-left corner
  3. Tap "Yale NetID (CAS) User Login"
  4. Enter your NetID and password; approve the Duo push notification on your phone
  5. Tap "Yes, this is my device" to skip repeat logins
  6. At the PWG check-in counter, show/scan the barcode on your screen
Screenshots

MTA TrainTime app — Metro-North tickets

Method A (by itinerary):

  1. Open the Trips tab
  2. Set origin/destination (e.g. Grand Central → New Haven)
  3. Tap the train you want
  4. Tap "Buy" in trip details

Method B (by ticket type):

  1. Open the Tickets tab
  2. Tap "Buy +"
  3. Choose trip type (one way, round trip, ten-trip)
  4. Select Peak/Off-Peak and check out
⚠️ Don't activate your ticket until you're on the train — activation can't be reversed. A peak ticket works for a peak-time train; an off-peak ticket on a peak-time train may require you to purchase again. Bikes/PEVs allowed space-permitting.
